A bit about me

I'm an IT professional looking for my next opportunity, but outside of work, I build things for fun. iOS and macOS apps, trading bots, browser games. If it seems worth making, I'll make it. I like solving real problems with code, and sometimes I solve imaginary ones too.

Apps & Projects

These are personal projects built on my own time. Mahj Nights is a free iPhone app on the App Store with an optional one-time unlock; the rest are not in production or offered commercially.

A selection of tools and applications I've designed and built to solve real IT challenges, and occasionally, just to have a bit of fun.

Mahj Nights

A free iPhone app that keeps score at your American mahjong table. Tap who won and it works out who pays whom, tracks the leaderboard, and settles everyone up at the end of the night. No account, ads or tracking. Free to try, with a one-time unlock.

App Store Website

Doodle Chess

A complete chess game drawn as cartoon doodles on notebook paper, in one HTML file with no frameworks. Four computer levels, 26 puzzles, PGN import and export, and nine papers. Plays offline.

Play it

AfterHours: Medspa AI Scheduler

A portfolio prototype of an AI voice assistant that books medspa appointments after hours, designed with HIPAA-style safeguards in mind. AfterHours handles Botox, HydraFacial, and laser bookings through natural conversation, sends SMS/email confirmations, and comes with a live calendar dashboard and admin panel. Powered by Claude AI, Twilio, Deepgram, and ElevenLabs.

Boop It

A browser-based twist on the classic Bop It toy. Hit the button on command before time runs out. Each round gets faster, more dramatic, and throws in spins, shakes, and confetti. Built for fun, playable anywhere.

Play it

News Digest

A macOS menu bar app that pulls RSS feeds across tech, AI, politics, and more, then uses Claude to write one clean daily digest with source links. Scheduled, sandboxed, and tested (59 unit tests, CI).

TaskFlow

A macOS task manager with Kanban, calendar, and Google Calendar integration in one place. Tasks support subtasks, priorities, tags, and recurring schedules, with a clean interface that stays out of your way.

Weather App

A Dark Sky-inspired desktop weather app with live forecasts, an hourly chart, 7-day outlook, minute-by-minute precipitation, and a live radar map. Built with React, TypeScript, and Electron — packaged as a native macOS app.

Stock Analyzer

Fetches live market data for stocks and crypto, calculates RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands, and outputs a composite BUY/SELL/HOLD signal. Includes a watchlist, multi-ticker comparison, and JSON export for deeper research.
